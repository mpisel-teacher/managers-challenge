The Manager's Challenge

Build the Best Team · Student Dashboard

Your mission: Choose the three employees who give your company the best chance of success. There is no single correct team. Be prepared to justify your decisions with evidence when you meet with the CEO.

No written explanations or PDF submission required. Your selections and notes save in this browser on this device.

Think about: What kind of workplace culture does this company have? Which two employee qualities matter most for this project? Be ready to explain your reasoning to the CEO.

3 · Meet the Employees

Review all six profiles. Every employee has strengths and weaknesses.

4 · Build Your Team

Select three different employees. You will explain your choices out loud during your CEO meeting.

5 · Probability of Success Simulator

Test how well your employees' strengths fit your company's project. You'll receive an estimated score, an explanation of what your team does well, and a potential concern. This is a fictional classroom simulation, not your grade.

Choose your team, then run the simulation.
Would you change your original team? You may keep your choices or try a different combination. The CEO will ask you to explain what you changed—or why you decided to stay with your original three.

These are optional notes for your meeting, not a written assignment.

6 · Get Ready for the CEO Meeting

Be ready to justify your decisions. The CEO may ask:

  • What kind of workplace culture does your company have? How do you know?
  • Why did you select these three employees?
  • Which employee is a Just Enougher, All-Star, or Climber? What evidence supports that?
  • How might your employees' strengths and weaknesses affect the company's success?
  • Did the simulation cause you to change your original team? Why or why not?
  • What if I suggest replacing one of your employees? Would you agree or defend your original choice?
CEO Challenge: You may be asked to compare your team with another employee option. Explain the benefits and consequences of both choices before making your decision.

You can keep this dashboard open during your CEO meeting. The PDF report includes your original team, every tested combination, score changes, and any employee selections changed between simulations. Click Create PDF Report to download a clean, organized PDF with a team summary and complete change history.
